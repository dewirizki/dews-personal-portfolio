Who I am

Dewi Rizki Fitriani is a Data Engineer based in Indonesia, working at the intersection of production data infrastructure and quantitative analysis. Since 2023 she has designed and maintained ETL pipelines across AWS (S3, Lambda, EC2, Athena, RDS) and Databricks, moving raw multi-source data into PostgreSQL and TimescaleDB for downstream analysis. That statistical grounding — validate before you ship — is what she brings to every pipeline she builds.

Data & analytics projects

Statistics on real questions

Where the Mathematics background shows up directly — correlation, clustering, and classification applied to real data.

Datathon Kecerdasan Artifisial 2021 — PPKM policy effectiveness Top 10 / 187

Team lead · National finalist · August 2021
Problem
Quantify whether Indonesia's PPKM social-restriction policy actually reduced workplace mobility and community transmission across Java's six provinces.
Approach
Merged Ministry of Health case/hospitalization/death data with Google COVID-19 Community Mobility data; ran Pearson correlation, then K-Means clustering validated by silhouette analysis.
Results
Confirmed cases vs. workplace mobility r = −0.52; hospitalizations vs. workplace mobility r = −0.88. Selected k = 4 clusters (silhouette score 0.514) segmenting provinces by transmission severity. DKI Jakarta's workplace mobility fell 52.20% versus baseline during Emergency PPKM.

PythonPearson correlationK-MeansSilhouette analysis

Mathematics thesis · Random Forest · 2019 Indonesian presidential election dataset
