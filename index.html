Datathon Kecerdasan Artifisial 2021 — PPKM policy effectiveness Top 10 / 187

Team lead · National finalist · August 2021
Problem
Quantify whether Indonesia's PPKM social-restriction policy actually reduced workplace mobility and community transmission across Java's six provinces.
Approach
Merged Ministry of Health case/hospitalization/death data with Google COVID-19 Community Mobility data; ran Pearson correlation, then K-Means clustering validated by silhouette analysis.
Results
Confirmed cases vs. workplace mobility r = −0.52; hospitalizations vs. workplace mobility r = −0.88. Selected k = 4 clusters (silhouette score 0.514) segmenting provinces by transmission severity. DKI Jakarta's workplace mobility fell 52.20% versus baseline during Emergency PPKM.

Fleet Telematics Platform — live GPS to sub-30s spatial queries

Independent build · Poetry monorepo · 2026
Problem
Fleet operators get a firehose of GPS pings but rarely turn it into what a dispatcher actually needs in seconds: where vehicle density is clustering right now, at block-level resolution, and which vehicles are behaving abnormally relative to the traffic immediately around them — not a fixed, context-blind speed threshold.
Approach
Normalized two structurally different live/replayed sources (GTFS-Realtime VehiclePosition feeds and NYC TLC trip records) into one canonical event schema published to Kafka/Redpanda. A PySpark Structured Streaming job (with a parallel PyFlink reference implementation) H3-indexes every event at resolution 9 (block-level) and its resolution-8 parent, computes 5-minute/1-minute-slide windowed density per cell, then runs a watermarked stream-stream join back onto the raw stream so each event is scored against its own local cell's current mean/stddev — flagging speed anomalies by z-score rather than a global threshold.
Data model
ClickHouse, spatial-key-first ordering (ORDER BY h3_r8, h3_r9, event_time) so a viewport+time query reads one contiguous range; a ReplacingMergeTree density rollup fed directly by Spark's foreachBatch; native H3 functions (h3kRing, h3ToParent) doing ring/neighbor expansion at query time only, since indexing was already done once at write time.
Tech stack
Kafka/RedpandaPySpark Structured StreamingPyFlinkUber H3ClickHouseFastAPIStreamlitGrafanaPoetry
Docs & code
PRD · Architecture doc · Streaming job · ClickHouse schema
What I learned
Where the math background pays off directly: a fixed speed threshold is a bad anomaly detector because "normal" varies by place and time of day — scoring against a rolling local mean/stddev per spatial cell (via a watermarked stream-stream join, not a simple aggregation) is what makes the alert statistically meaningful instead of noisy.
